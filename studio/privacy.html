Privacy Policy

Story Studio · Effective date: October 23, 2026

Story Studio (the "App") is a private tool that publishes videos to YouTube channels owned by its operator ("we"). Contact: agardius001@gmail.com.

1. Who uses the App

Only the channel owner signs in, with the Google accounts of the owner's own channels. The App is not offered to the public and does not create accounts for anyone else.

2. What Google data the App accesses

  • Basic information about the signed-in channel (channel ID and title).
  • The channel's own videos and playlists, so it can upload, schedule and organize them.
  • The channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, traffic sources).

The App does not read e-mail, contacts, files or any data of viewers or other users.

3. How the data is used

Solely to publish our own videos and to plan our upload schedule. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

4. Storage and retention

Sign-in tokens and statistics reports are stored on a private server controlled by the operator. Tokens are kept until access is revoked; reports are deleted when no longer needed.

5. Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account › Third-party connections. Requests to delete stored data can be sent to the contact address above.

6. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
