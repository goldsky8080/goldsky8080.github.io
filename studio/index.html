Story Studio

A private publishing tool for our own YouTube channels

Story Studio is an internal tool run by an independent creator. It produces narrated videos and publishes them to YouTube channels that we own and operate, then reads those channels' statistics so we can plan our schedule.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos, thumbnails and descriptions to our own channels, and schedules when they go public.
  • Organizes our own videos into playlists.
  • Reads view and audience statistics of our own channels (YouTube Analytics).

It is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in, and only to the owner's own channels.

Story Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
